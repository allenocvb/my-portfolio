# Reading/Watching List 📕🎥

Books and shows I'm currently reading or have recently finished that have influenced how I think, build, and grow.

# Books
### Technical
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** by Martin Kleppmann
  - Understanding of how real-world systems handle scale, reliability, and trade-offs.
- **System Design Interview - An Insider's Guide** by Alex Xu
  - How to structure high-level system thinking and communicate design decisions clearly under constraints.
### Non-Technical
- **The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy** by Douglas Adams
  - Creativity, humor, and misadventures of the last surviving Earth man. Logic isn't the only thing when thinking about complex systems. 42..yes 42.
- **The Alchemist** by Paulo Coelho
  - Finding meaning beyond the superficial and valuing connections.
- **Vagabond(manga)** by Takehiko Inoue
  - Fictionalized account of the legendary Japanese swordsman Miyamoto Musashi. Queue the beautiful and peaceful manga panel of Musashi after brutually killing 60 people and saying something like "You can't rewrite the past, but you can stop rereading it."
- **Ramayana** Valmiki
  - Ancient sanskrit epic. Gave insights into Hinduism, emphasizing heroism and virtue, or dharma. Read in lit class. 

# Shows
- **One Piece**
  - Greatest anime of all time..currently on egg head arc. It's not long enough.
- **Twin Peaks**
  - Mystery that values atmosphere, ambiguity, and intuition over clean answers.
- **Dan Da Dan Season 2**
  - Fun fast-paced storytelling that blends humor, horror, romance, and emotion.
- **Bleach**
  - A classic shonen about growth, resolve, and identity-finally completing the last of the Big Three.
- **Jujutsu Kaisen**
  - Explores power, sacrifice, and moral trade-offs in a world where strength always comes at cost. Excited for new season.
